Spa Pedicure Benefits and Aftercare Tips

1 Million Master Technicians 15 Aug, 2026 3 min read
Discover what a professional spa pedicure includes and how simple aftercare keeps feet soft, comfortable and beautifully polished.

A professional spa pedicure combines careful nail grooming with exfoliation, hydration and a polished finish. It is a relaxing way to maintain well-presented feet throughout the year.

What does a spa pedicure include?

Your appointment may include cleansing, nail shaping, cuticle care, gentle hard-skin work, exfoliation, massage and your chosen polish.

Benefits of regular pedicures

  • Keeps toenails neatly shaped.
  • Softens dry surface skin.
  • Provides relaxing time away from a busy routine.
  • Offers long-lasting colour for open-toe shoes.

How often should you book?

Many clients book every four to six weeks. Your ideal schedule depends on nail growth, footwear, product choice and how quickly dry skin returns.

Pedicure aftercare

Moisturise feet regularly, keep them clean and dry, and avoid cutting cuticles at home. Wear comfortable footwear after your appointment.

When a salon pedicure is not appropriate

A beauty pedicure does not diagnose or treat medical conditions. Seek qualified healthcare advice for pain, infection, open wounds or ongoing concerns.
